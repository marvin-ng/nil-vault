The Problem:

480,000 college athletes became small business owners overnight when the NCAA opened NIL in 2021. Most of them have no agent, no advisor, and no system. Contracts live in DM threads. Deadlines get missed. Tax season is a surprise. The compliance office finds out when it's already a problem.
NIL Vault is the back office they never got.

What It Does:

Every brand deal in one pipeline, from first inquiry to final payment.

The 5-Stage Deal Pipeline:
INQUIRY > NEGOTIATING > SIGNED > POSTED > PAID

Features
Deal Pipeline: Kanban board with drag-and-drop across 5 stages
Deadline Tracker: Color-coded urgency: Red / Yellow / Green
Contract Storage: Upload and store contracts per deal
Payment Tracker: Log every payment. Know who still owes.
Tax Estimator: SE tax + federal bracket + quarterly IRS dates
FTC Compliance Checker: Flags missing #ad or #sponsored before you post
Compliance PDF Export: One-click report for the athletic department
